# Canadian National Guard

Welcome to the public repository for the Canadian National Guard 

We are a civilian funded, civilian run network of Canadians advocating for freedom and a return of truth, liberty and justice within Canada.

We exist to protect Canadians from political tyranny enabled by fake currency used by government to steal from and control citizens.



3 pillar mandate: Educate, Protect, Advocate



Policy advocacy: 3 non negotiable changes demanded by WE THE PEOPLE

1. End the fraud of currency inflation, adopt a sound money standard in Canada

2. End income tax, property tax, capital gains tax and carbon taxes

3. Form a Justice TaskForce (JTF) to track every tax dollar spent since 2010, investigate politicians who broke trust and form a court system free from political influence to punish politicians who commit fraud or treason.
